# Understanding risk and return

Investing involves uncertainty. In general, investments with the possibility of
higher returns also expose an investor to a greater chance of loss. That
relationship does not predict what any particular investment will do.

## Compare choices carefully

- Identify the time horizon and purpose of the money.
- Consider how much loss could be tolerated without changing essential plans.
- Review costs, liquidity, diversification, and the reliability of the source.

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission provides a general
[introduction to investing](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing).
This lesson is educational and does not recommend buying, selling, or holding
any investment.
