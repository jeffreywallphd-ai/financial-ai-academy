# Education Versus Financial Advice

- Status: proposed
- Canonical for: initial financial-claims boundary

## Product Posture

The platform teaches concepts and supports educational simulations. It must not present generalized lessons, adaptive learning, model output, or simulated portfolio results as personalized investment advice or a suitability determination.

## Guardrails

- Separate educational learner personalization from investment personalization.
- Label simulations, hypothetical results, assumptions, limitations, fees, and data periods.
- Do not convert a learner profile into a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold a security.
- Ground factual financial explanations in reviewable sources and show uncertainty where appropriate.
- Do not promise performance or imply that course completion predicts investment success.
- Route advice-like features, live execution, brokerage connectivity, or suitability inputs to an explicit decision and qualified review.

Detailed wording, jurisdictional application, disclosures, and review workflows remain decision-required.
