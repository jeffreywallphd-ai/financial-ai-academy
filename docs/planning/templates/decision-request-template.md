# Decision Request: Short Question

## Decision Needed

State one choice in terms that a named human decision-maker can approve.

## Why Now

Identify the capabilities, slices, work packets, contracts, or risks blocked by the choice and the consequence of deferring it.

## Current Authority and Constraints

Link current canonical sources, readiness entries, non-goals, risk constraints, provider terms, and facts that all viable options must preserve.

## Options

| Option | Benefits | Costs and risks | Contracts and operations affected | Reversibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Recommendation

Provide a reasoned recommendation when evidence is sufficient. Separate verified facts, assumptions, and inference.

## Evidence Required

List discovery, prototype, legal/product review, security analysis, provider-term review, or operational qualification needed before approval.

## Required Approver

Identify the role or authority required to decide; an agent cannot self-approve.

## Decision Record and Promotion

Record the approver, date, selected option, and rationale. Link the resulting ADR or owning canonical document and decision-readiness update; this request does not replace them.

## Dependent Planning Updates

List artifacts that must be reshaped or unblocked after the accepted decision is recorded canonically.

## Approval History

Record explicit authorized decisions and canonical promotion without treating this request as decision authority.
