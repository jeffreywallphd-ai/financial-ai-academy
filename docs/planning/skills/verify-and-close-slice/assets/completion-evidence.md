# Completion Evidence Checklist

- Delivered outcome and affected boundaries are identified.
- Applicable `AGENTS.md` and repository-root `docs/README.md` were read and followed.
- Every required packet is complete and linked.
- Every acceptance scenario has owning evidence.
- Exact focused and repository-level commands and results are recorded.
- Failure, denial, malformed, timeout, degraded, replay, stale-version, provenance, parity, accessibility, or theme evidence is included when applicable.
- Contract and schema compatibility is recorded.
- Canonical documentation and derived context are synchronized.
- Migration, rollback, backup, or recovery evidence is recorded when applicable.
- Qualification not performed is explicit.
- Assumptions, residual risks, gaps, and follow-up work are explicit.
- Completion acceptance remains pending until an authorized human decides.
