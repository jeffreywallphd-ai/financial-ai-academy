# Context Pack: Product and Domain

## Use When

Work affects scope, users, editions, non-goals, domain language, or capability ownership.

## Preserve

- The platform is educational and simulation-oriented.
- Community and cloud profiles share semantic behavior.
- Initial domain/module names are hypotheses until validated.
- Product scope does not silently cross into brokerage, custody, suitability, or guaranteed outcomes.
