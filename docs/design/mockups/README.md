# Interface Mockups

- Status: directional reference
- Canonical: no
- Derived production baseline: [Interface Style Guide](../style-guide.md)

## Purpose

These mockups communicate the intended visual character, broad information hierarchy, major page concepts, and the relationship between light and dark themes for Financial AI Academy.

## Interpretation Rule

These images are **general guidelines, not exact implementation guides**. They must not be treated as executable specifications, acceptance criteria, pixel-perfect layouts, or authoritative definitions of product behavior.

The mockups may guide:

- the warm ivory, deep forest green, and restrained brass visual direction;
- the premium editorial tone and typography hierarchy;
- broad page composition and feature grouping;
- consistent light and dark theme treatment; and
- early discussion of learner workflows and navigation.

The mockups do not define:

- exact copy, labels, numbers, dates, sample users, provider names, or financial data;
- final navigation, component dimensions, responsive breakpoints, or interaction behavior;
- accessibility conformance, validation rules, calculations, AI behavior, or adaptive-learning policy;
- API, event, provider, persistence, or domain contracts;
- entitlement, pricing, licensing, compliance, or commercial-edition behavior; or
- final brand marks, icons, illustrations, or production-ready design assets.

Generated content in the images is illustrative and may be inaccurate or internally inconsistent. Implementation decisions must come from accepted product, domain, architecture, risk, accessibility, and contract documentation. When a mockup conflicts with a canonical source, the canonical source governs.

For production colors, spacing, layout, cards, charts, themes, and iconography, use the [Interface Design System](../README.md) and its executable assets rather than sampling values or extracting icons from these images.

## Design Review Expectation

Before any mockup is promoted into an implementation plan, review its page purpose, workflow, responsive behavior, accessibility, data provenance, education-versus-advice boundary, and local/cloud applicability. Record approved requirements in the appropriate canonical documentation rather than modifying an image and treating it as authority.
