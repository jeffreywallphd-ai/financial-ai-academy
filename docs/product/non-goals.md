# Product Non-Goals

- Status: accepted
- Canonical for: scope exclusions during foundation work

The current project does not aim to provide:

- live securities trade execution,
- custody of customer funds or securities,
- personalized investment suitability determinations,
- guaranteed returns or performance claims,
- autonomous AI control of grades, permissions, portfolios, or financial records,
- provider-specific market-data semantics as the internal domain model,
- different local and cloud domain implementations,
- microservices before extraction evidence,
- a general plugin marketplace before trust and governance decisions,
- regulatory, legal, accreditation, or professional-certification claims,
- production business logic implemented only in notebooks.
