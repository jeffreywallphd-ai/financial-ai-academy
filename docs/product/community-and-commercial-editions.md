# Community and Commercial Editions

- Status: proposed
- Canonical for: edition relationship and commercialization posture

## Shared Core

The local open-source and managed-cloud editions share domain rules, application operations, contract versions, migrations, provider interfaces, and portable export formats.

## Community Baseline

The intended community baseline includes core curriculum, assessment, adaptive-learning baseline, portfolio simulation, local backup/export, free-data provider integration, and local or externally configured model providers.

## Commercial Differentiation

Managed infrastructure, organization administration, enterprise identity, billing, premium licensed providers, scalable jobs, governance controls, operational qualification, analytics, and support may be commercial capabilities.

Commercial differences must be expressed through adapters, entitlements, operational services, and separately reviewed modules rather than a contradictory domain fork.

## Licensing Decision

The repository is currently GPLv3. The effect of that license on hosted services, extension distribution, proprietary modules, contributor agreements, or dual licensing is decision-required and needs qualified legal review. Architecture documentation must not imply a licensing model that has not been accepted.
