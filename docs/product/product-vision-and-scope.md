# Product Vision and Scope

- Status: proposed
- Canonical for: current product intent

Financial AI Academy will help learners build financial-investing, quantitative, AI, and ML capability through structured curricula, practical simulations, assessments, and adaptive learning pathways.

## Intended Capabilities

- Competency-oriented curricula and prerequisites
- Lessons, examples, exercises, projects, and assessments
- Evidence-backed learner progress and mastery projections
- Adaptive next-activity recommendations with explanations
- AI-assisted tutoring and grounded content interaction
- Canonical market-data access through replaceable providers
- Simulated portfolios, valuation, and backtesting
- Financial AI/ML datasets, experiments, and evaluation
- Local open-source operation and a managed cloud offering

## Potential Major Pages

The following working page concepts describe the intended learner experience. They are a directional capability map, not an implementation specification, contractual commitment, or delivery sequence.

| Page | Potential purpose and features |
| --- | --- |
| Learner Dashboard | Continue learning, recommended next activity, goals, competency summary, recent work, and educational market context |
| Learning Path | Individualized curriculum map, prerequisites, completion states, optional topics, and explanations for recommendations |
| Course and Module Overview | Objectives, prerequisites, lesson outline, expected effort, required labs, assessments, and projects |
| Lesson Workspace | Structured lesson content, examples, notes, knowledge checks, citations, and a grounded AI tutor |
| Assessment and Practice | Diagnostic and formative practice, scenario questions, feedback, competency evidence, and targeted review |
| Market Data Lab | Provider-neutral market-data exploration, charts, metrics, comparisons, provenance, quality, and dataset export |
| Portfolio Sandbox | Simulated portfolios, allocations, benchmarks, rebalancing, risk metrics, stress tests, and reflections |
| AI and ML Lab | Guided financial-model experiments, features, evaluation, leakage and overfitting checks, model cards, and reproducibility |
| Projects and Notebooks | Guided or open-ended projects, analytical notebooks, datasets, milestones, saved results, and submissions |
| Progress and Competencies | Mastery views, evidence history, strengths, review opportunities, achievements, and portable learner records |
| Learning Library | Searchable courses, lessons, labs, and projects with topic, level, duration, prerequisite, and availability filters |
| My Workspace | Saved notes, charts, datasets, simulations, notebooks, projects, bookmarks, and AI conversations |
| Data Sources and Integrations | Provider connections, local imports, coverage, quotas, health, attribution, licensing reminders, and local/cloud availability |
| Profile and Preferences | Learning goals, experience, topics, accessibility, themes, AI-assistance preferences, notifications, privacy, and data export |
| Help, Glossary, and Methodology | Platform help, financial and AI/ML terms, adaptive-learning methods, data provenance, AI limitations, and education-versus-advice guidance |

The [interface mockups](../design/mockups/README.md) illustrate these concepts but remain noncanonical. Production presentation follows the [interface design system](../design/README.md).

## Potential Cross-Cutting Features

- Evidence-backed adaptive pathways with deterministic eligibility and policy checks
- Grounded AI tutoring with citations, limitations, and learner-controlled assistance preferences
- Provider-neutral market, model, content, storage, identity, and job integrations
- Transparent provenance for financial data, datasets, AI output, recommendations, simulations, and learning evidence
- Search, bookmarking, notes, saved work, export, backup, and portable learner records
- Reproducible portfolio, backtesting, quantitative, AI, and ML experiments
- Accessible light, dark, and system themes with responsive learner experiences
- Shared semantic behavior across local community and managed-cloud deployments
- Clear separation of observed data, simulations, model predictions, and AI-generated interpretation

## Potential Instructor and Administrative Experiences

- Curriculum, lesson, assessment, project, and competency authoring workflows
- Instructor review of learner evidence, projects, feedback, and approved overrides
- Cohort and organization learning views with appropriate privacy and authorization controls
- Organization membership, roles, entitlements, enterprise identity, governance, and support for managed deployments
- Provider, model, content, data-license, usage, audit, and operational-health administration

These supporting experiences depend on unresolved content-format, identity, tenancy, entitlement, provider, licensing, and commercial decisions. They must be refined through product discovery and accepted decisions before implementation.

## Initial Users

- Individual learners
- Instructors and curriculum authors
- Administrators of learning organizations
- Contributors building content, providers, and analytical tools

## Scope Boundaries

The platform is educational. It is not initially a brokerage, investment adviser, trade-execution system, credentialing authority, or guarantee of investment outcomes. Any future crossing of those boundaries requires explicit product, legal, security, and architecture decisions.
