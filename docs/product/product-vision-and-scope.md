# Product Vision and Scope

- Status: proposed
- Canonical for: current product intent

Financial AI Academy will help learners build financial-investing, quantitative, AI, and ML capability through structured curricula, practical simulations, assessments, and adaptive learning pathways.

## Intended Capabilities

- Competency-oriented curricula and prerequisites
- Lessons, examples, exercises, projects, and assessments
- Evidence-backed learner progress and mastery projections
- Adaptive next-activity recommendations with explanations
- AI-assisted tutoring and grounded content interaction
- Canonical market-data access through replaceable providers
- Simulated portfolios, valuation, and backtesting
- Financial AI/ML datasets, experiments, and evaluation
- Local open-source operation and a managed cloud offering

## Initial Users

- Individual learners
- Instructors and curriculum authors
- Administrators of learning organizations
- Contributors building content, providers, and analytical tools

## Scope Boundaries

The platform is educational. It is not initially a brokerage, investment adviser, trade-execution system, credentialing authority, or guarantee of investment outcomes. Any future crossing of those boundaries requires explicit product, legal, security, and architecture decisions.
